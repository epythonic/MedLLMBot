How the bot uses the price list

The catalog is the clinic's services or the shop's products. With every client question, the bot reads the active items and passes Claude lines of the form “Name — price lei (description)”. An edit is visible from the next message, no restart is needed. When answering about a price, the bot adds that the administrator will confirm the exact cost.

Large catalog: search instead of the whole price list

As long as there are no more active services than the Catalog in prompt, up to items threshold in {{ section_link('bot-config') }} (50 by default), Claude gets the whole price list with every question — this is faster and cheaper. If there are more services, the whole price list does not fit in the request, and the bot searches the catalog for what it needs: the model picks the words itself (“teeth cleaning”, “implant”), gets up to 10 matching items with prices and answers based on them.

  • The search covers the name and description and understands different word forms: “cleanings” finds “Cleaning”.
  • What the bot searched for and how much it found is shown in {{ section_link('audit-event') }}, in the What the bot searched for field. If a client asked about a service and the search did not find it, add the words clients use for it to its name or description.
  • An answer with search takes two to four calls to Claude instead of one, so it costs a little more and takes longer.

Protection against made-up prices

If the model names an amount in lei that is not in the knowledge base, the client will not see it: they will get “I'll check the price with the administrator and get back to you”, and a record with the outcome “Price replaced” will appear in {{ section_link('audit-event') }}. If there are many such records, check that all prices are in the price list.

How to work with services

  • Add: the + New Item button above the table → fill in the name and price → Save.
  • Change a price: the pencil icon in the service's row → Price, lei → Save.
  • Remove temporarily (the service is not offered right now): clear the Active checkbox. The bot will stop offering it, but the record stays — later you only need to switch it back on.
  • Delete permanently: the trash icon in the row. Deletion cannot be undone.
  • Change the order: drag the row by the handle in the Sort order column — the new order is saved right away, and the bot sees it from the next question. Dragging works when the list has no search or filters. You can also do it by hand: the Sort order number in the form, lower means higher up.

How to fill it in

  • One service — one row with one price. The price is a whole number in lei.
  • If the price depends on the case, enter the minimum and write in the description “price from; the doctor gives the exact price after an examination”.
  • The description is for facts: duration, what the price includes. Do not write instructions for the bot in it.
  • The bot does not make up promotions. If there really is a promotion, add it to the service description.
