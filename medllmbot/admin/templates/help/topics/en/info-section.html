What this section is

General information about the clinic or shop for the bot. Each section is a title and text; the bot sees them together with {{ section_link('service') }} and {{ section_link('faq-item') }}. If none of these sections has the answer to a question, the bot says that the administrator (in the shop, the manager) will answer during business hours.

What to write here

  • Address, landmarks, how to get there, parking.
  • Phone numbers and opening hours — without them the bot cannot tell the client when they will be called back.
  • For a shop — delivery, pickup, warranty and returns.
  • Payment methods, installments, guarantees.
  • General rules: how to prepare for an appointment, what to bring, conditions for children.

What not to write here

  • Commands to the bot (“always offer a discount”, “answer only in Romanian”). Write facts.
  • Personal data of doctors and patients, internal phone numbers.
  • Information you are not sure about: the bot will repeat it to the client as a fact.
{% call note("info", "The bot's rules of behavior are set by the developer") %} The bot answers only about its own company, does not change its role and does not name prices that are not in the knowledge base. Domain rules depend on the profile: the dental clinic does not make diagnoses, the shop does not promise availability (see {{ section_link('bot-config') }} → “Business profile”). These rules are built into the code and take priority over the knowledge base text — they cannot be changed here. {% endcall %}

Order of sections

  • Change the order: drag the row by the handle in the Sort order column — the new order is saved right away, and the bot sees it from the next question. Dragging works when the list has no search or filters. You can also do it by hand: the Sort order number in the form, lower means higher up.
