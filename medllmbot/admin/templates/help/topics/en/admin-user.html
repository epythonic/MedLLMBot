Who is here

Extra admin panel accounts: for example, a partner or a manager who shows the demo to clients. Each person logs in with their own email and password, and one person's access can be turned off without changing anyone else's password.

The owner is not in the list: their login and password are set in the server settings (ADMIN_USERNAME and ADMIN_PASSWORD), so the owner can be neither turned off nor deleted from this section.

Who is signed in is shown in the top right corner, next to the language switcher: the email or login and the role, Owner or Administrator. The profile menu has My account (for an administrator from this section) and Logout.

How to add an administrator

  1. Click + New Administrator.
  2. Enter the email — it is the login. Upper or lower case does not matter.
  3. Choose a password of at least 12 characters and click Save. The account is active at once.
  4. Hand over the email and password in person or in a secure messenger, not in a group chat.

On the login page the administrator types the email into the Username or email field.

How to turn off access or change the password

  • Turn off: open the record with the pencil icon, clear the Active box and save. All open sessions of this administrator end at once, and the record stays — access can be restored with the same box.
  • Change the password: type a new password into the Password field when editing. The administrator's old sessions end. An empty field keeps the current password.
  • Delete — if the access will not be needed any more.
