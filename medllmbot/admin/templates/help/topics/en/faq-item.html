How the bot uses the FAQ

All enabled questions and answers are part of the bot's knowledge base. The bot does not copy an answer word for word: it answers in its own words and in the client's language, Russian or Romanian. An edit takes effect from the next message.

How to write good answers

  • One question, one record. Phrase the question the way clients ask it: “Does dental treatment hurt?”
  • Keep the answer short and factual: what, how much, when. The bot will shorten a long text anyway.
  • Do not promise what the clinic does not do: the bot will repeat the promise to the client.
  • Prices are easier to keep in {{ section_link('service') }}. If an answer does contain a price, the bot treats it as current.

How to work with questions

  • Add: the + New Question button above the table → question and answer → Save.
  • Edit: the pencil icon in the row.
  • Disable temporarily: untick Enabled.
  • Change the order: drag a row by the handle in the Sort order column — the new order is saved right away, and the bot sees it from the next question. Dragging works when the list has no search or filters. You can also set it by hand: the Sort order number in the form, the smaller the number, the higher the row.
