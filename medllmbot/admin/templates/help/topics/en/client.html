Where clients come from

A client appears here as soon as they first write to the bot. With every next message the bot updates their name, username and Last message time. You cannot add or delete a client by hand: their conversation and leads would disappear with them.

How to block a client

  1. Find the client: the search box searches by Chat ID, username and name. The Chat ID is easy to copy from {{ section_link('audit-event') }} or from a lead.
  2. Click the pencil icon in the client's row.
  3. Tick Blocked and click Save.

A faster way is right from {{ section_link('audit-event') }}: on a record's page, next to the Chat ID, there is a Block client button. To unblock, untick the box here or click Unblock on the log record's page. The change applies from the client's next message; there is no need to restart the bot.

What happens to a blocked client

  • The bot silently stops replying. The client gets no message about the block.
  • Claude is not called, so a blocked client's messages cost nothing.
  • No leads are created from their messages.
  • Each message still goes to {{ section_link('audit-event') }} with the outcome “Client blocked” — you can see whether they keep writing.

When to block

Spam, insults, repeated attempts to hack the bot (many log records marked Suspicious). The Blocked filter on the right shows all blocked clients.

{% call note("warning", "One suspicious phrase is not a reason") %} The attack detector sometimes makes mistakes. Before blocking, open the client's log and make sure it is not an ordinary question phrased in an unusual way. {% endcall %}

Live operator

A client can ask for a person instead of the bot: “I want to talk to a human”, «позовите менеджера», “vreau să vorbesc cu un om” or the /operator command. Then:

  1. The bot tells the client it has passed the question on, and you get a Telegram notification with the latest messages of the dialog and a Back to bot button.
  2. While you have the dialog, the bot stays silent in this chat: the client's messages, including photos and voice messages, come to you, and Claude is not called. The With operator until column shows until when.
  3. To answer, reply in Telegram (swipe or “Reply”) to the bot's message about this client: the notification, a forwarded message or the notification about their lead. Your text, photo or file goes to the client, and 👍 appears on your message. Each reply extends the period; replying to an old notification turns operator mode on again.
  4. When you are done, click Back to bot: the client gets a message that the bot is answering again. If you do not reply at all, the bot comes back on its own after the period set in {{ section_link('bot-config') }}. The client can also return to the bot on their own with the /start command; you will get a message about it.

In {{ section_link('audit-event') }} they appear with the outcomes “Asked for operator”, “Sent to operator” and “Operator reply”.
