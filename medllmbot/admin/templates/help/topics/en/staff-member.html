Who is here

Clinic or shop employees who work with clients in Telegram: a manager, a consultant, a coordinator. The bot sends each of them live operator calls and new leads, and each can answer clients right from Telegram, like the owner.

The owner is not in the list: their Telegram ID is set in the server settings (ADMIN_ID). The owner gets everything, including alerts about suspicious messages and the spending limit; staff do not get those. The bot writes to all of them in the language from the Administrator language field in {{ section_link('bot-config') }}.

How to add a staff member

  1. Ask the person to open the bot in Telegram and press “Start”. Without it the bot cannot write to them.
  2. Find them in {{ section_link('client') }}: the number in the Chat ID column is their Telegram ID.
  3. Here, click + New Telegram staff member, enter the name and the Telegram ID and save. From the next message the bot treats this person as staff.

How calls are shared

  • A client asks for a live person: the owner and every staff member on the list get the notification.
  • The first one to reply handles the client: the others get “Client @ivan is handled by Anna”, and from then on only that person gets the client's messages.
  • Another staff member's reply does not reach the client: the bot tells them who handles the client. To take the dialog, for example when a colleague is away, press Back to bot under a message about this client and reply again.
  • Leads with a phone number go to everyone: the owner and the staff.

How to turn off

Open the record with the pencil icon, clear the On the team box and save, or delete the record. From the next message the bot stops sending this person calls and leads and does not pass their replies to clients. If they were handling a dialog, every operator gets the client's messages again.

{% call note("warning", "By the Telegram ID only") %} The bot recognizes a staff member by the numeric Telegram ID that Telegram itself puts into every message. Anyone can copy a name, photo or @username, so the bot ignores them. A staff member sees the conversations of clients who call the operator: add only people you trust. {% endcall %}
