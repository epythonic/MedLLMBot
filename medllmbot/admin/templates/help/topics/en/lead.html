Where leads come from

Leads are created by the bot; they are not added manually. When a client sends a message with a phone number, for example +373 60 000 000 or 060000000, the bot:

  • saves a lead with the status New;
  • sends the administrator a Telegram notification “New lead #N” with the name, phone number and message text;
  • replies to the client as usual.

The lead is created even if Claude is unavailable at that moment or the message is longer than the limit in {{ section_link('bot-config') }}: the bot will ask the client to write more briefly, but will save the number and tell the client so. There will be no lead only if the client is blocked or writes too often. The Telegram notification contains the first 500 characters of the message; the full text is in the Message column.

How to handle a lead

  1. Open the section — new leads are at the top. To see only unprocessed ones, in the Filters block choose Status → New.
  2. Call the client at the number in the Phone column. The Message column often already has the service or product they need and a convenient time.
  3. Change the status right in the list: the Status column has a drop-down list, and the choice is saved at once — the frame lights up green for a second. You can change the status as many times as needed: “In progress” → “Done”.
  4. To record what you agreed on, click the pencil icon and fill in Administrator note, then click Save.

New leads without reloading

While the section is open, it checks every 15 seconds whether new leads have appeared. If they have, a banner “New leads: N” appears above the table — click Show to refresh the list. The Telegram notification still arrives as usual.

Statuses

NewThe bot created the lead; no one has contacted the client yet.
In progressYou have contacted the client or are waiting for their reply.
DoneThe client is booked for an appointment or the order is placed — the lead is closed successfully.
RejectedThe client changed their mind, does not answer the phone, or this is not a lead.

Search

The search box above the table searches by client name, phone number and message text. The client's whole conversation can be read in {{ section_link('chat-message') }}: filter it by Chat ID from the lead page.
