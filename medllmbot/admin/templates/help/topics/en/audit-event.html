What is recorded

Every incoming message, including rejected ones: what the client wrote, what the model answered, what the client finally received, how much it cost and whether there were signs of an attack. The log is read-only.

Attack signs

The detector looks in the client's message for typical tricks used to take control of the bot, and in the model's answer for fragments of its rules. The signs that fired are shown in the Attack signs column.

What happens to a suspicious message

  • The client gets the model's usual answer: the bot is protected from hacking by its rules and answer checks, not by the detector. The exception is an answer with fragments of the rules: it is replaced with a neutral phrase.
  • The message and the answer are not saved in {{ section_link('chat-message') }}, so the attack does not build up in the bot's memory.
  • The administrator gets a Telegram notification in Russian, «Внимание: подозрительное сообщение…» (“Warning: suspicious message…”), at most once every 15 minutes per client, so an attacker cannot flood them with messages.

How to investigate a suspicious message

  1. In the Filters block, choose Suspicious → Yes.
  2. Open the record with the eye icon. Compare Model answer and Sent to client: if they differ, the bot replaced a dangerous answer.
  3. Look at the client's other messages: the Chat ID filter here or in {{ section_link('chat-message') }}.
  4. If the attempts repeat, click Block client next to the Chat ID on the record page and confirm. The client can be unblocked there too.

New events without reloading

While the log is open, it checks for new records every 15 seconds. A banner “New log events: N” appears above the table; if some of them are suspicious, the banner turns yellow and shows how many. Click Show to refresh the list.

{% call note("warning", "The detector is a signal, not a defense") %} The detector works on phrase patterns: an attacker can rephrase, and an ordinary client can accidentally write something that looks like an attack. Do not block anyone because of a single flag. The built-in patterns are set by the developer; if you notice a new trick that the detector misses, pass the example on to the developer. {% endcall %}

How long the log is kept

As many days as set in {{ section_link('bot-config') }} → Keep log, days (90 by default). The bot deletes old records at startup and then every 6 hours. Costs are not lost: the daily totals stay in {{ section_link('usage-daily') }}.
