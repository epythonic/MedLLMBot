Where the data comes from

The page is computed when you open it, from {{ section_link('audit-event') }} and {{ section_link('lead') }} for the selected period: 7, 30 or 90 days, in UTC. Claude is not called for the analysis: analytics costs nothing, and client conversations are not sent anywhere else.

  • Question — a client's text message that reached the bot. Not counted: the greeting (/start), photos and voice messages, messages skipped by the rate limit or because the client is blocked, messages with attack signs (those are reviewed in {{ section_link('audit-event') }}), and the conversation with the live operator: a person handles it, not the bot.
  • Asked for an operator — how many times clients asked for a live person. If it grows, the bot is missing something: look at “Unanswered questions” and at {{ section_link('client') }}.
  • The log is kept for as many days as set in {{ section_link('bot-config') }}. If the period is longer, analytics covers only the retention period.

Dialogs and leads

  • Dialog — one client's messages without a break longer than a day. A client who comes back a week later starts a new dialog.
  • Dialog with a lead — the client left a phone number in it, and a lead appeared in {{ section_link('lead') }}. The share of such dialogs shows how often a conversation with the bot leads to a call.

How topics are determined

A topic is found by keywords in the question, in Russian and Romanian (and some English): «цена», “preț” and “price” all mean “Prices”. A question can fall into several topics: “How much does a cleaning cost?” is both “Prices” and “Hygiene and whitening”.

  • Left a phone number — messages with no known words but with a phone number: this is usually how a client answers the bot's request to leave contact details.
  • Other — everything else, including short replies like “Thanks”. The examples under the name hint at which topic is missing.
  • Under each topic there are up to three latest questions. Messages with a phone number are not shown as examples.

Unanswered questions

This is a hint at what to add to the knowledge base. Identical questions are grouped into one row, the most frequent at the top, up to 50 rows per page. Technical failures — a Claude API error, the spending limit, a message that is too long — are not listed here: they are not a gap in the knowledge base, and you can see them in {{ section_link('audit-event') }}.

What to do with an unanswered question

  1. Click the question: the log record opens with the model's answer and what the client received.
  2. If the question is asked often, add an answer: a typical question to {{ section_link('faq-item') }}, information about the business to {{ section_link('info-section') }}, a service or product to {{ section_link('service') }}.
  3. The bot sees the changes from the next message. Once it answers the same question, the row disappears from the list; otherwise it drops off when it falls outside the period. The “Unanswered” card at the top counts all unanswered questions for the period, including those the bot answered later.
