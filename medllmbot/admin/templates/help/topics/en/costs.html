How to read the report

  • One card is one month, newest at the top. The card header shows the total for the month.
  • Rows are models, most expensive at the top. The last row, “Total”, is the sum across all models.
  • For the current month, a month forecast is shown next to the total: the amount spent is divided by the days that have passed and multiplied by the number of days in the month. In the first days of the month the forecast is rough.
  • Months are counted in UTC, as in Anthropic's invoices.

This is an estimate, not an invoice

The report is calculated from the tokens in Claude's answers and the prices in {{ section_link('model-price') }}. Taxes, contract discounts and API requests made outside the bot are not taken into account. For the exact amount, see the Anthropic Console, in the Usage / Cost section.

{% call note("warning", "The “no price” warning") %} If there is a warning about requests with no price under a month's table, the total is too low: add the model to {{ section_link('model-price') }}. {% endcall %}

How to reduce costs

  • Model. Haiku is several times cheaper than Sonnet and Opus and handles questions about the knowledge base well.
  • History depth in {{ section_link('bot-config') }}: each history message adds tokens to every client question.
  • Knowledge base size. It is sent with every question. The cache makes repeats cheaper, but unnecessary sections and inactive services are better switched off.
